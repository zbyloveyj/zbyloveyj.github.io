## Editorial descriptions and caveats

- **Global gut ecology:** 20,178 metagenomes, 36 countries, more than 50 disease states are dimensions of the *Advanced Science* cohort study, not the author's own recruitment total.
- **BIGHI:** Presented as an observational research cohort framework. Cross-system associations are not causal evidence.
- **Multi-kingdom MDD:** Exploratory disease discrimination and microbiome associations are not clinically approved diagnostic tools.
- **CNS / breast cancer / animal model papers:** No individual clinical recommendations or causal assertions have been added to short summaries.
- **Tools:** CLEAR, normative ecological network modeling and BioAgentLab are public research/teaching projects, not asserted as clinically validated software.
