01 / PERSPECTIVE

从微小的生命，
理解更大的世界。Small organisms.
A larger perspective.

我的研究处在微生物生态、脑科学与计算生物学的交叉处。我关注的不只是某种微生物增加或减少，更想知道：群落结构如何变化，这些变化与人的临床表现、认知和脑功能有什么联系。My work sits at the intersection of microbial ecology, brain science and computational biology. Beyond whether a particular microbe increases or decreases, I ask how communities change and how those changes relate to clinical traits, cognition and brain function.

为此，我将宏基因组、多组学和脑影像放在一起分析，也关注方法的可解释性与跨队列可重复性。一个有吸引力的结果，还需要经得起不同数据、不同分析条件的检验。I bring metagenomics, multi-omics and neuroimaging into a shared analysis, with an emphasis on interpretability and reproducibility across cohorts. A compelling result still needs to hold up under different data and analytical choices.

从问题出发Question first先想清楚要回答什么。Be clear about what the study asks.
让证据说话Evidence matters区分观察、解释与推测。Distinguish observation from interpretation.
把方法讲清楚Make it reproducible让分析能够被理解与复查。Make analysis understandable and checkable.

03 / WRITING & LIFE

写科学，
也写科学里的人。Writing science.
Remembering its people.

我以「一心问道」为笔名写作。小说让我走近发现发生时的普通人；教材和手册，则把复杂知识与科研经验整理成可以分享的内容。I write as 一心问道. Fiction lets me explore ordinary lives at the moment of discovery; textbooks and guides turn complex knowledge and research experience into something that can be shared.
