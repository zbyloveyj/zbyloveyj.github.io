01 / RESEARCH APPROACH

01 / 03

Research, across scales.跨尺度研究。

My work begins with ecological variation in microbial communities and asks how robust patterns can be related to human phenotypes and brain function—without assuming causality from association.从微生物群落的生态差异出发，研究可复现的模式如何与临床表型及脑功能关联，同时明确统计关联与因果机制之间的距离。

RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Questions before methods.先有科学问题，再选择方法。

01 / MAP

Ecological structure微生物生态结构

Community composition, variability and ecological factors across cohorts and environments.比较不同队列和环境中的群落组成、变异性与连续生态因子。

02 / LINK

Clinical heterogeneity临床异质性

Evaluate associations with symptoms, cognition and phenotypic diversity.探索微生物生态特征与症状、认知和临床表型差异的统计关联。

03 / CONTEXT

Brain signatures脑功能与空间特征

Integrate neuroimaging and spatial reference maps with transparent evidence boundaries.整合脑影像与空间参考图谱，明确跨数据集解释所受的证据限制。

04 / TEST

Robustness & validation稳健性与独立验证

External data, sensitivity analyses and limitations as part of the result.将外部数据验证、敏感性分析及局限性纳入研究结果本身。

012026 / ADVANCED SCIENCE

A continuous picture of global gut ecology.从全球宏基因组识别连续生态结构。

Wiredancer applied a similarity-constrained factor model to 20,178 metagenomes from 36 countries and over 50 disease states. The study reported three overlapping ecological factors and tested their reproducibility across cohorts.Wiredancer通过相似性约束的生态因子建模分析20,178份来自36国、50余疾病状态的宏基因组数据，识别重叠的连续微生物生态因子，并检验其跨队列复现性。

032026 / JOURNAL OF AFFECTIVE DISORDERS

Beyond bacteria in depression.抑郁症微生物组研究不止于细菌。

A metagenomic study of first-episode drug-naïve depression profiled bacteria, archaea, viruses and microbial eukaryotes, and assessed clinical associations plus the exploratory discriminatory potential of combined features.在首发未用药抑郁症队列中联合分析细菌、古菌、病毒和微型真核生物，评估其临床相关性及联合特征的探索性区分潜力。

RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

A good model should travel beyond its training set.好的模型，应当走出训练数据。

External validation, confounding, missingness and measurement differences are not an appendix to biological discovery. They determine what the evidence can support.独立验证、混杂因素、缺失值与测量差异并不是生物学发现之后才考虑的附加问题，而是决定研究证据能够支持何种结论的关键。
