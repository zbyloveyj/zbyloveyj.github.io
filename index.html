微生物生态、脑肠关联与算法建模Microbial ecology, brain–gut research & computational modeling

我是华南理工大学博士研究生，研究微生物群落如何组织，以及这些生态变化与精神疾病、认知和脑功能之间的联系。我将宏基因组、脑影像与临床表型结合，使用生态因子分析、网络建模和可解释机器学习，从不同层级理解复杂生物系统。I am a PhD candidate at South China University of Technology. My research examines the organization of microbial communities and their associations with psychiatric disorders, cognition and brain function. I integrate metagenomics, neuroimaging and clinical phenotypes using ecological factor analysis, network modeling and interpretable machine learning.

Wiredancer：从全球宏基因组识别连续生态结构Wiredancer: continuous structure in the global gut microbiome

我们分析来自36个国家的20,178份宏基因组，通过相似性约束的因子建模，研究微生物群落是否呈现连续、重叠的生态结构，并检验这些结构在不同队列中的复现性。We analyzed 20,178 metagenomes from 36 countries using similarity-constrained factor modeling, examining continuous, overlapping ecological structure and its reproducibility across cohorts.

BIGHI：连接临床、微生物组与脑影像的队列框架BIGHI: a cohort framework linking clinical profiles, microbiome and neuroimaging

脑肠健康计划将精神疾病症状、认知评估、微生物组与脑影像纳入同一个前瞻性队列框架，为研究脑与肠道之间的关联提供系统的数据基础。The Brain–Gut Health Initiative brings psychiatric symptoms, cognitive assessments, microbiome profiles and neuroimaging into a prospective cohort framework for brain–gut research.
