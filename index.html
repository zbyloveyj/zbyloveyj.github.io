微生物生态、脑肠关联与算法建模Microbial ecology, brain–gut research & computational modeling

我是华南理工大学博士研究生，研究微生物群落如何组织，以及这些生态变化与精神疾病、认知和脑功能之间的联系。我将宏基因组、脑影像与临床表型结合，使用生态因子分析、网络建模和可解释机器学习，从不同层级理解复杂生物系统。I am a PhD candidate at South China University of Technology. My research examines the organization of microbial communities and their associations with psychiatric disorders, cognition and brain function. I integrate metagenomics, neuroimaging and clinical phenotypes using ecological factor analysis, network modeling and interpretable machine learning.

我的工作从算法出发，连接微生物生态与临床问题；也通过开放代码、科学写作和教材分享研究实践。I develop computational methods to connect microbial ecology with clinical questions, and share research practice through open code, science writing and educational guides.

Advanced Science · 2026 第一作者First author

Wiredancer：从全球宏基因组识别连续生态结构Wiredancer: continuous structure in the global gut microbiome

我们分析来自36个国家的20,178份宏基因组，通过相似性约束的因子建模，研究微生物群落是否呈现连续、重叠的生态结构，并检验这些结构在不同队列中的复现性。We analyzed 20,178 metagenomes from 36 countries using similarity-constrained factor modeling, examining continuous, overlapping ecological structure and its reproducibility across cohorts.

这项工作的重点是以连续生态因子描述群落变化，让不同人群和疾病状态能够在共同的生态表示中比较。算法、群落结构与跨队列验证构成了同一条分析主线。Continuous ecological factors provide a shared representation for comparing populations and disease states, bringing algorithm design, community structure and cross-cohort validation into one analysis.

Research · 2026 共同第一作者Co-first author

BIGHI：连接临床、微生物组与脑影像的队列框架BIGHI: a cohort framework linking clinical profiles, microbiome and neuroimaging

脑肠健康计划将精神疾病症状、认知评估、微生物组与脑影像纳入同一个前瞻性队列框架，为研究脑与肠道之间的关联提供系统的数据基础。The Brain–Gut Health Initiative brings psychiatric symptoms, cognitive assessments, microbiome profiles and neuroimaging into a prospective cohort framework for brain–gut research.

这类研究需要把不同测量对应到同一个体，并结合临床差异解释跨系统关联。队列设计与数据对齐是后续建模的基础，也是理解患者异质性的重要前提。Relating measurements to the same individual and interpreting associations in their clinical context are central to this framework. Cohort design and data alignment support subsequent modeling of patient heterogeneity.

Journal of Affective Disorders · 2026 共同第一作者Co-first author

多界微生物组：从细菌扩展到完整的肠道生态视角Multi-kingdom microbiome: beyond bacteria in depression

在首发未用药抑郁症队列中，我们联合分析细菌、古菌、病毒和微型真核生物，研究多个微生物界的特征变化、临床相关性及联合特征的探索性区分潜力。In first-episode, drug-naïve depression, we jointly profiled bacteria, archaea, viruses and microbial eukaryotes, examining clinical associations and the exploratory discrimination offered by combined features.

研究将关注点从单一微生物界扩展到跨界特征整合，比较不同界提供的信息及其互补性，为进一步理解疾病相关的群落变化提供线索。The study extends analysis beyond a single microbial kingdom, examining the complementary information in multi-kingdom profiles as a basis for understanding disease-associated ecological variation.
