RESEARCH

Research directions.研究方向。

One question, multiple scales: how do we make sense of complex microbial ecosystems and their relationships with human health?以同一个问题连接多个尺度：如何理解复杂微生物生态系统及其与人类健康的关系？

Microbial ecology at scale大规模微生物生态建模

Continuous ecological factors, healthy reference systems and reproducible patterns across populations, countries and disease states.研究跨人群、跨国家与多疾病状态的连续生态因子、健康参考系统及其可复现模式。

RESEARCH / 02

Microbiome–brain associations微生物组与脑功能关联

Integrating gut metagenomes with cognition, psychiatric symptoms and multimodal brain imaging, without mistaking association for causation.整合肠道宏基因组、认知、精神疾病临床症状与多模态脑影像，谨慎解释跨尺度关联。

RESEARCH / 03

Multi-kingdom microbiomics跨界微生物组研究

Studying bacteria, archaea, viruses and microbial eukaryotes together to understand disease-associated shifts beyond bacteria alone.联合分析细菌、古菌、病毒和微型真核生物，刻画超越细菌组的疾病相关变化。

Bacteria / Archaea / Viruses / Eukaryotes

Trustworthy AI for biology可验证的生物学人工智能
